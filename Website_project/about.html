Our Story

Payag ni Parekoy began in 2021 during a time when people were staying at home and restaurants were quiet. Founded by Edsel “Parekoy” Delgado, the business started as a simple food stall in a small kubo (payag), serving only takoyaki and milkshakes.

As the pandemic continued, more people discovered their food. The small kubo quickly became popular, leading them to expand into a bigger space and offer more meals and food packages.

Today, Payag ni Parekoy serves a wide variety of food while staying true to its roots—affordable, delicious, and made with passion.
